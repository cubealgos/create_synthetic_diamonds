# Gallery

Taken by Kevin in the 1.0.0+26.2 client on the release build, later. This mod has no screen of its
own, so every shot favors what makes the mechanism legible: the press mid-cycle, the input and
output items, the sorted piles. Files under `gallery/`, in the order they appear on the Modrinth
page; the first is the featured image. Captions go into Modrinth's gallery description field.

| # | File | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-press-over-depot.png` | A mechanical press over a depot, charcoal feeding in from a hopper above. |
| 2 | `02-diamond-moment.png` | The press cycle completing with a diamond in the output, the rare 0.5% roll caught mid-pop. |
| 3 | `03-belt-sorter.png` | A belt line running the press's output through a filtered splitter, flint, gunpowder and diamonds peeling off into separate chests. |
| 4 | `04-coal-block-press.png` | A coal block going into the press, its by-product output (9 flint or 9 gunpowder per miss) beside a single-item press for comparison. |
| 5 | `05-compact-farm.png` | A compact charcoal farm — a tree farm feeding a smoker line into the press — the whole slow-diamond setup in one small footprint. |

Retake after any recipe or model change.
